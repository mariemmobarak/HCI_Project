Memory Game Help

  • How to Play: Click on cards to flip them and find matching pairs!
  • Moves: Each pair of card flips counts as one move.
  • Timer: Try to match all pairs as quickly as possible!
  • Match: When two cards match, they stay flipped.
  • Restart: Click the "Restart" button to play again.
  • Tip: Remember where cards are to match them faster!
  • Return: Click the logo (top left) to go back to dashboard.
